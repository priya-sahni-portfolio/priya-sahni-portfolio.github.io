ALLEGIS GLOBAL SOLUTIONS / BANKING

Rebuilding the case for a rate review

A rejected recommendation became an opportunity to strengthen the analysis and rebuild stakeholder confidence.

BenchmarkingInternal equityStakeholder communication
The question

How could a Canadian banking client update rates that had remained unchanged for roughly four years?

My contribution

I rebuilt the analysis using two independent market sources, compared results side by side and checked internal equity and competitor rates. I translated an approximately 10% market gap into a targeted 5% recommendation.

The result

The revised recommendation was accepted. The work reinforced the value of transparent methodology and a focused, defensible recommendation.

ALLEGIS GLOBAL SOLUTIONS / ENERGY

Making supplier costs more competitive

Turning inconsistent supplier submissions into a comparable view of rates, outliers and opportunities for improvement.

Data validationSupplier analysisCost optimization
The question

Which supplier rates were above market, and where could the client act?

My contribution

I gathered supplier inputs through an RFI, cleaned and validated submissions, reviewed outliers and compared rates by job title and level against market benchmarks.

The result

The review supported rate reductions across 29 job titles. Some rates had been as much as 40% above market. I focused the recommendations on specific, evidence-backed opportunities.

Bringing clarity to complex rate cards

Creating a consistent basis for comparing supplier and direct-payroll pricing structures.

Rate-card strategyStandardizationExcel analysis

How could different rate structures be compared fairly?

My contribution

I separated supplier and direct-payroll components and standardized supplier maximum bill rates to make comparisons more consistent.

The result

The client had a clearer rate-card structure for evaluating pricing decisions. This example demonstrates my approach to making complex commercial data understandable.
